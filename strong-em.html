Bold & Italic: <strong> & <em>

<strong> marks important text (bold). <em> marks emphasized text (italic). Both carry semantic meaning for screen readers.

💡 TipSemantic difference: <b> is visually bold with no meaning. <strong> signals importance. Same for <i> vs <em>.
