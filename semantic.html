Semantic Elements

HTML5 semantic tags give meaning to your structure. Use <header>, <nav>, <main>, <article>, <aside>, <footer> instead of nameless divs.

💡 TipWhy semantics? Screen readers and search engines use these tags to understand page structure, improving accessibility and SEO.
